Help

Quick answers. If yours isn't here, tap the chat button in the corner and ask.

How do I add an expense?

Open Add, pick a category, give it a name, type the cost and choose daily or monthly. Or tap a preset on the same page and it's added straight away.

Why are daily costs multiplied by 30.44?

That's the average number of days in a month. It lets a $5 daily coffee and a $15 monthly subscription sit on one monthly scale, so the totals are fair.

What do the budget colours mean?

On the dashboard, green means you've used under half of your monthly budget, amber means 50 to 89 percent, and red means 90 percent or more. Set the budget on your profile.

What is the 10-year number?

It's what your monthly total could grow to if you invested it each month at 7% a year instead of spending it. It's an estimate to make a point, not a promise.

Where is my data kept?

Only in this browser, using localStorage. Nothing is sent anywhere. If you clear your browser data, or open the site on another device, your account won't be there.

Where is the calculator?

It's the round button just left of the chat button, bottom-right of every page. Tap it, use the keys or your keyboard, and tap it again to close.

What can the chat helper answer?

How to add and edit expenses, what the dashboard numbers mean, the budget colours, accounts, dark mode and the calculator. It matches keywords, so short questions work best. Try "how much do I spend?" once you're signed in.
