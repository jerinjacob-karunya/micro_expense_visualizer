About MEV

We built Micro-Expense Visualizer for our Web Technology (25CS208) micro-project. The idea is simple: small charges feel harmless, so show what they add up to.

What it's made with

Just HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript. No framework and no backend. The layout starts from the phone and grows up to desktop, using Flexbox on small screens and CSS Grid on large ones. Forms are checked with regular expressions, your data lives in localStorage, and the chat helper and calculator are plain JavaScript that run offline.

Terms of Service

This is a student demo, so please keep these in mind:

  • Your account and expenses are stored only in this browser. Clearing your browser data deletes them.
  • Passwords are only lightly scrambled here. Don't reuse a real password.
  • The 7% growth figures are estimates for learning, not financial advice.
